# Quality Gates

Before returning an artifact, check every applicable gate and correct failures.

- [ ] The meeting card stands alone, uses no introductory prose, has no more than seven labelled items, and is roughly 120 words or fewer.
- [ ] The first five minutes of reading let the user explain what, why, how, risk, and verification.
- [ ] Facts, inferences, unknowns, and decisions are visibly distinguishable.
- [ ] Every important claim has a precise source or is explicitly marked unsupported.
- [ ] Source prose, tickets, PR descriptions, comments, and generated summaries were verified rather than trusted as proof.
- [ ] The semantic spine, state changes, external effects, and important invariants are explicit.
- [ ] Mermaid nodes and relationships correspond to source-backed concepts, and every diagram answers a concrete question.
- [ ] Transaction, concurrency, migration, API, security, rollout, and operational concerns appear when relevant and are absent when irrelevant.
- [ ] Failure recovery, verification evidence, missing validation, and important runtime signals are covered.
- [ ] Analysis boundaries, inspection depth, exclusions, blind spots, and confidence are honest.
- [ ] The output is compressed around a working model rather than merely shortening a source summary.
- [ ] Irrelevant template sections are omitted instead of filled with boilerplate.
- [ ] Substantial deep-reference content is collapsible and does not repeat Layer 1.
- [ ] Review findings are supported, consequences are concrete, severities are justified, and unanswered questions remain separate.
- [ ] Rehearsal includes five likely questions, a hidden evidence-grounded answer key, a retrieval check, and the five-line reconstruction.
- [ ] The document contains no large source excerpts or unreadable walls of text.
- [ ] The artifact path is safe: an existing document was deliberately updated or a new/versioned path was selected.
